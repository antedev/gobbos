# Roles

*Goblins do not have careers. They have jobs they took by force and roles they fill to avoid being eaten. A Boss is only as strong as their gang's fear of them, and that fear changes depending on what the Boss can do.*

A [[Role]] represents the tactical expectation of your **Goblin Boss** based on their current [[Main Stats]] configuration. Your [[Role]] dictates how you command your [[Mob]] and what special abilities you can use. 

Because you increase your [[Main Stats]] by spending [[XP]] during downtime, your [[Role]] is dynamic. As your stats change, your [[Role]] can shift, allowing you to adapt your playstyle between raids.

---

## 1. Determining Your Role

Your [[Role]] is determined by your **Primary Stat** (your highest [[Main Stat]]) and your **Secondary Stat** (your second-highest [[Main Stat]], which also determines your maximum [[Grunt]]):

*   **Specialist Roles (Gap of 2 or more):** If your highest stat is **2 or more levels higher** than your second-highest stat, you are a **Specialist** (e.g. Tough Specialist, Slink Specialist).
*   **Hybrid Roles (Gap of 1 or 0):** If the gap between your highest and second-highest stat is **1 or 0**, you are a **Hybrid** combining both stats.

>> **IMPORTANT:** If you have multiple stats tied for highest or second-highest, you choose which of those stats acts as your Primary and Secondary for the purpose of your [[Role]].

---

## 2. Role Levels and Grunt-Gating

Your **Role Level** is always equal to your current maximum [[Grunt]] (ranging from **Level 1 to Level 5**). Your level determines which [[Quirk|Quirks]] from your [[Role]] you can access:

*   **Level 1 (Grunt 1+):** Unlocks your **Role Vibe** (a passive utility trait).
*   **Level 3 (Grunt 3+):** Unlocks your **Signature Quirk** (a powerful active ability).
*   **Level 5 (Grunt 5):** Unlocks your **Master Quirk** (a legendary capstone).

### Grunt-Gating (Morale Lock)
Because goblins only follow leaders who look strong, your active [[Role]] abilities are **Grunt-Gated** in combat. 
*   To activate or benefit from a Level 3 or Level 5 [[Role]] [[Quirk]], your **current Grunt** must be equal to or higher than the quirk's level.
*   If your **current Grunt** drops below the quirk's level (due to fumbles, failed tests, or mob rebellions), that ability is instantly **locked** and cannot be used until you regain [[Grunt]] (e.g., by defeating enemies or asserting dominance).

### The Specialist Ceiling (Maximum Level 3)
Because [[Main Stats]] cap at Level 5 (reaching Level 6 triggers retirement), a Specialist can never have a second-highest stat of 4 (since the highest would need to be 6). 
*   Therefore, **it is mathematically impossible to be a Specialist at Grunt 4 or Grunt 5.**
*   At Grunt 4, the gap must shrink to 1 (Tough 5 / Grunt 4), naturally forcing you to transition into a Hybrid [[Role]].
*   **The Balancing Rule:** To compensate for this ceiling and the loss of Level 5 capstones, **Specialist Roles receive significantly more explosive, high-impact Level 1 and Level 3 abilities** than Hybrid Roles.

---

## 3. Downtime Transition Protocol

If your stats change during the [[Lair Phase]] (downtime) due to spending [[XP]] or acquiring mutations, your [[Role]] may shift:

1.  **Vibe Swap:** Your old Level 1 Vibe is removed, and your new Role Vibe is added immediately for free.
2.  **Quirk Retention:** Any equipped active quirks from your old [[Role]] are unequipped and placed in your reserves. They cannot be used while in your new [[Role]].
3.  **Equipping New Quirks:** You may fill your standard slots of 3 [[Personal Quirks]] (see [Quirks & Twists](14_Quirks.md)) with your new Role's quirks, spending [[XP]] to purchase them during downtime if you do not already know them.

## 4. The 16 Roles

### Tough Roles (Primary: Tough)
*   **The Meat-Wall (Tough Specialist):** A massive, iron-skinned brute who relies entirely on physical mass, ignoring commands to absorb punishment.
*   **The Raider (Tough + Slink):** A swift shock trooper who charges into the fray, strikes hard, and scuttles away.
*   **The Enforcer (Tough + Mouth):** A brutal squad leader who beats enemies with weapons and beats allies with whips to keep them fighting.
*   **The Iron-Tinker (Tough + Brains):** A heavy crafter who builds heavy scrap-shields and swings volatile, customized bludgeons.

### Slink Roles (Primary: Slink)
*   **The Scuttler (Slink Specialist):** A silent stalker who moves through the shadows like a ghost, completely uncatchable but commands no respect.
*   **The Gut-Cutter (Slink + Tough):** A vicious backstabber who relies on speed to flank enemies and stab them in vulnerable joints.
*   **The Ring-Leader (Slink + Mouth):** A slippery agitator who distracts guards and directs mobs from safety.
*   **The Saboteur (Slink + Brains):** An infiltration expert who bypasses locks, triggers traps, and throws toxic flasks.

### Mouth Roles (Primary: Mouth)
*   **The Over-Lord (Mouth Specialist):** A loud, self-important tyrant who wears flashy hats and orders squads from a distance but avoids personal danger.
*   **The Taskmaster (Mouth + Tough):** A cruel commander who pushes runts past their breaking points, treating mobs as renewable resources.
*   **The Swindler (Mouth + Slink):** A fast-talking coward who uses lies and panic-shouts to redirect attacks onto other goblins.
*   **The Chant-Monger (Mouth + Brains):** A loud speaker who channels magic tags through rhythmic chants, keeping mobs focused.

### Brains Roles (Primary: Brains)
*   **The Sage-Tinker (Brains Specialist):** A wild scientist who locks themselves in the workshop to brew acids and build unstable mechanical contraptions.
*   **The Runecaster (Brains + Tough):** A magically active brute who carves spell tags onto their weapons and armor to boost physical combat.
*   **The Hex-Weaver (Brains + Slink):** A sneaky spell-slinger who casts volatile magic from the shadows and hides behind full cover.
*   **The Shaman (Brains + Mouth):** A tribal mystic who talks to spirits, commands elemental tags, and coordinates magical raids.

## 5. Example Role Progression Tracks

### The Sage-Tinker (Brains Specialist — Brains - Grunt >= 2)
*   **Level 1 (Vibe):** *Unstable Genius.* When crafting custom gear, your Crafting Capacity is treated as **1 higher** than your [[Brains]] level. You also get +1d on all tests to identify oddities or spell runes.
*   **Level 3 (Signature Quirk):** *Farkle-Snatcher [Cost: 1 Grunt].* When you roll a [[Brains]] check and roll one or more 1s (potential fumbles), you can choose to ignore the failure. Instead, you lock those 1s to instantly channel them into a random magic burst dealing 2 [[Grit]] damage to all targets in your zone.

### The Raider (Tough + Slink Hybrid — Tough - Slink <= 1)
*   **Level 1 (Vibe):** *Parkour-Slash.* Crossing zone barriers or low cover during a Move action does not reduce your movement speed.
*   **Level 3 (Signature Quirk):** *Vaulting Strike [Cost: 1 Action, 1 Grunt].* Make a Move action directly into an enemy's zone, passing through any minor cover, and immediately resolve a melee attack with **+2d Attack Pool**.
*   **Level 5 (Master Quirk):** *Unstoppable Momentum.* If you score a success on a melee attack after moving at least 2 zones in the same round, you can immediately make a free Move action or knock the target **Prone**.

### The Enforcer (Tough + Mouth Hybrid — Tough - Mouth <= 1)
*   **Level 1 (Vibe):** *Meat Shield Mastery.* When you use a [[Mob]] as a meat shield, the [[Mob]] ignores the first 1 damage dealt to it.
*   **Level 3 (Signature Quirk):** *Bully Command [Cost: 1 Grunt].* Give an order to a [[Mob]] in your zone. The [[Mob]] immediately gains **+1d Attack Pool** on their next action, but takes 1 damage from your whip/kick.
*   **Level 5 (Master Quirk):** *Reign of Terror.* Any enemy [[Mob]] in your zone must pass a Morale test (5+) at the start of combat or instantly scurry into an adjacent zone (Flee).

### The Swindler (Mouth + Slink Hybrid — Mouth - Slink <= 1)
*   **Level 1 (Vibe):** *Slippery Words.* You get +1d on Mouth tests to talk your way out of trouble or distract guards.
*   **Level 3 (Signature Quirk):** *Look Over There! [Cost: 1 Grunt].* Force an enemy in your zone to turn their back. Your allies get **+2d Attack Pool** against that enemy until the end of the round.
*   **Level 5 (Master Quirk):** *Scapegoat [Cost: 1 Grunt].* When you are targeted by an attack, you can redirect the attack to an adjacent player's Boss.
