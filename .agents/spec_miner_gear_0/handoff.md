## Edge Cases

| # | Feature | Input | Observed Behavior |
|---|---------|-------|-------------------|
| 1 | Stagger on Partial Hit | Attacker rolls 1 success against a Defence 2, Size 2 enemy with a Heavy Melee weapon (+1 Impact Size). | Attacker base Size 1 + 1 Heavy = Impact Size 2. Since Impact Size (2) $\ge$ Target Size (2), the partial hit successfully inflicts `Staggered` on the enemy until Round Closure (-1 Defence TN). |
| 2 | Stagger Mass Resistance | Attacker rolls 1 success against a Defence 3, Size 3 Troll with a Medium Melee weapon (Impact Size 1). | Attacker Impact Size is 1. Target Size is 3. Since Impact Size (1) $<$ Target Size (3), the Troll's mass resistance completely ignores the Stagger effect. No condition is applied. |
| 3 | Clatter Roll: Clean Dodge | Enemy attacks Boss with Threat `5+/2`, Damage 3. Boss rolls Slink 3d6 and scores two 5s (2 successes) on stat dice, plus one 6 on armor dice. | Stat dice scored 2 successes, meeting Threat TN 2. Clean Dodge achieved: Boss takes **0 Damage**! Armor dice are disregarded. |
| 4 | Clatter Roll: Failed Dodge with Armor Mitigation | Enemy attacks Boss with Threat `5+/2`, Damage 3. Boss rolls Slink 2d6 scoring one 5 (1 success, fails Threat TN 2) and Medium Armor 2d6 scoring one 5 and one 6 (2 successes). | Dodge fails (1 $<$ 2 TN). Armor dice score 2 successes on 5+. Damage taken = $3 - 2 = 1$ Grit damage deducted from Boss. |
| 5 | Clatter Roll: 0 Saved Actions | Enemy attacks Boss with Threat `5+/1`, Damage 2. Boss spent all 3 Standard Actions on their turn and has 0 saved actions. Boss wears Heavy Armor (+3d Armor Dice). | Boss cannot roll Slink Stat Dice (0 active evasion). Boss rolls 3d6 Armor Dice. If 2 successes are rolled on 5+, incoming 2 damage is completely mitigated (0 Grit lost). |
| 6 | Ablative Gear Sacrifice | Boss at 1 Grit is hit for 2 unmitigated damage by an enemy warhammer (would drop Grit to -1 / death). Boss holds a Spiked Pot-Lid Shield. | Boss declares Gear Sacrifice. The shield shatters instantly and is removed from inventory. ## 2. Logic Chain
1. **Weapon and Stagger Model:** Weapons add dice or modify ranges/capabilities without post-roll math bloat. Melee weapons have an Impact Size. On a partial hit ($\ge 1$ success but $<$ Defence TN), if $\text{Impact Size} \ge \text{Target Size}$, `Staggered` condition (-1 Defence TN on enemies, -1d Dodge/Parry on PCs) is applied until Round Closure. If Impact Size $<$ Target Size, target mass resistance negates the Stagger. Heavy weapons (+1 Impact Size) and Crushing weapons (+2 Impact Size) allow goblins to stagger larger foes (Size 2 and Size 3).
2. **Armor and Clatter Defense Model:** Enemies attack deterministically with `Threat [Face]+/[TN]` and flat `Damage`. The defender executes a single Clatter Roll: Stat Dice (Slink for Dodge, Tough for Shield Parry) vs Threat TN. If Stat Dice succeed $\rightarrow$ 0 Damage. If Stat Dice fail (or 0 saved actions) $\rightarrow$ Armor Dice mitigate damage by 1 per 5+. Medium Armor grants +2d armor but imposes Bane 1 on Slink; Heavy Armor grants +3d armor but imposes Bane 2 on Slink; Shields grant +1d armor and unlock Tough Parry. Desperate Bosses can trigger Ablative Gear Sacrifice to destroy armor/shield and negate lethal damage.
3. **Mob Gear and Swarm Dynamics:** Mobs carry gear with scaled bulk: Mob Armor costs $\text{Size} \times \text{Armor Bulk}$ (e.g. Size 3 Mob in Medium Armor takes 6 Bulk). Casualties leave dead goblins' gear behind, avoiding mid-combat encumbrance overflow. Mobs use shared tools at flat Bulk. Damage to Mobs is tracked on d6 health dice starting at 6; single-target damage decrements dice with spillover; AoE/Cleave damage hits ALL dice in the pool simultaneously. Mobs defend via passive armor dice or the reactive "Scatter!" order (Boss Mouth vs Threat TN + Size penalty; Gamble failure causes 1 Trample damage to all dice, loot drop, Out of Control, and Boss Stagger).
4. **Consumables & Explosives Model:** Explosives use standardized Area Threat Profiles (`Threat [Face]+/[TN]`, Damage, `[Tags]`) affecting all units in the blast Zone. Impact Size equals Tier (T1=1 to T5=5), resolving against Bosses (Clatter Roll), Mobs (Scatter or all-dice damage), Standard enemies (instant kill if Threat TN $\ge$ Defence TN), and Boss/Elite enemies (1 Wound + Stagger if Impact Size $\ge$ Size).
5. **Modular Boss Quirks / Talents Model:** Built upon the Unified Modular Powers System. Meat Shield redirects damage to an allied Mob in the Zone; Ankle Bite provides a free counter-attack with +1 Success on a clean Dodge; Second Wind / Push Luck spends 1 Grunt to reroll non-1 dice on any test.
